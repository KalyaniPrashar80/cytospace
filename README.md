# CYTO SPACE

An interactive 3D web experience built for anyone who's ever only seen a cell as a flat, boring circle with arrows pointing at it from a school biology textbook.

Built as an immersive way to explore cellular machinery like a living space.

---

###  What's Inside
* *Cinematic Scroll & Zoom:* Scroll down from a single dot into a fully crowded, drifting 3D cell interior.
* *Textbook vs. Reality Toggle:* Switch instantly between a flat school-worksheet view and the living 3D environment.
* *Interactive Molecular Hotspots:* Click around to inspect key cellular players up close:
  * *Kinesin:* The two-footed protein walking along microtubule tracks.
  * *Ribosome:* The molecular machine translating mRNA into protein chains.
  * *ATP Synthase:* The biological turbine motor spinning to generate energy.
  * *Membrane Receptor:* Docking signals and sending glowing pulses inward.
* *Coding Analogies:* Every organelle comes with a practical developer analogy (because a nucleus is basically a private repo and a ribosome is a compiler).
* *Speed of Life Controls:* Speed up or slow down the cellular hustle in real-time.
